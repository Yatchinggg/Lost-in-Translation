# Daily Communication in Thailand

## Project: Lost in Translation — AI Thai Language Helper

## Purpose

This document helps foreigners greet people, express thanks, apologize, and ask for clearer communication in everyday situations.

## Common Phrases

The phrases below use basic forms. Add an appropriate polite ending when speaking.

| English | Thai | Situation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hello. | สวัสดี | Greeting someone. |
| Thank you. | ขอบคุณ | Expressing thanks. |
| Sorry / Excuse me. | ขอโทษ | Apologizing or getting attention. |
| It is all right / No problem. | ไม่เป็นไร | Reassuring someone. |
| I do not understand. | ไม่เข้าใจ | Explaining a communication difficulty. |
| Could you speak more slowly? | พูดช้าลงหน่อยได้ไหม | Asking someone to slow down. |
| Could you say that again? | พูดอีกครั้งได้ไหม | Asking for repetition. |
| Can you speak English? | พูดภาษาอังกฤษได้ไหม | Asking about English communication. |
| Could you write it down? | ช่วยเขียนให้หน่อยได้ไหม | Asking for written information. |
| Could you help me? | ช่วยหน่อยได้ไหม | Requesting help. |

## Polite Endings

The ending relates to the speaker, not the person being addressed.

### Common male-speaker forms

- สวัสดีครับ — Hello.
- ขอบคุณครับ — Thank you.
- พูดอีกครั้งได้ไหมครับ — Could you say that again?

### Common female-speaker forms

- สวัสดีค่ะ — Hello.
- ขอบคุณค่ะ — Thank you.
- พูดอีกครั้งได้ไหมคะ — Could you say that again?

Female speakers commonly use ค่ะ for statements and requests, and คะ for questions.

## Context and Meaning

### ขอโทษ

This can mean “sorry” after a mistake or “excuse me” when politely getting someone's attention.

### ไม่เป็นไร

This can mean “it is all right” or “no problem.” Its meaning depends on the conversation, so it should not automatically replace every English use of “never mind.”

### ช่วยหน่อยได้ไหม

This is a general request for help. If possible, explain what kind of help is needed.

## Example Conversation

Visitor: ขอโทษครับ ไม่เข้าใจครับ
English: Sorry, I do not understand.

Visitor: พูดช้าลงหน่อยได้ไหมครับ
English: Could you speak more slowly?

Visitor: ช่วยเขียนให้หน่อยได้ไหมครับ
English: Could you write it down?

Visitor: ขอบคุณครับ
English: Thank you.

## Example AI Response

User: Someone is speaking too fast. What should I say?

Thai: ขอโทษค่ะ พูดช้าลงหน่อยได้ไหมคะ

English: Excuse me. Could you speak more slowly?

Usage: Use this when you need the other person to slow down. A male speaker can say ขอโทษครับ พูดช้าลงหน่อยได้ไหมครับ.

## How This Supports the Project

The assistant can help users manage basic conversations and recover from misunderstandings.

It should:
1. Identify the user's intention.
2. Provide a natural Thai phrase and its English meaning.
3. Explain the appropriate polite ending.
4. Provide both common forms when the speaker's preference is unknown and the distinction is useful.
5. Explain that any English pronunciation guide is approximate because Thai tones matter.
