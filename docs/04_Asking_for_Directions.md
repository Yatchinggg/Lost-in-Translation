# Asking for Directions in Thailand

## Project: Lost in Translation — AI Thai Language Helper

## Purpose

This document helps foreigners ask where places are, request directions, and understand simple replies.

## Questions for Finding Places

Add an appropriate polite ending: ครับ for male speakers, or ค่ะ / คะ for female speakers depending on the sentence.

| English | Thai | Situation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where is the restroom? | ห้องน้ำอยู่ที่ไหน | Finding a restroom. |
| Where is the BTS station? | สถานีรถไฟฟ้าบีทีเอสอยู่ที่ไหน | Finding a BTS station. |
| Where is the MRT station? | สถานีรถไฟฟ้าเอ็มอาร์ทีอยู่ที่ไหน | Finding an MRT station. |
| How do I get to [place]? | ไป[สถานที่]ยังไง | Asking how to reach a destination. |
| Is it far from here? | ไกลจากที่นี่ไหม | Asking about distance. |
| Can I walk there? | เดินไปได้ไหม | Asking whether walking is possible. |
| Could you point it out on the map? | ช่วยชี้ในแผนที่ให้หน่อยได้ไหม | Asking for help with a map. |
| Is it this way? | ไปทางนี้ใช่ไหม | Checking the direction. |

Replace [สถานที่] with the destination name.

## Words the User May Hear

| Thai | English |
| --- | --- |
| ตรงไป | Go straight. |
| เลี้ยวซ้าย | Turn left. |
| เลี้ยวขวา | Turn right. |
| อยู่ทางซ้าย | It is on the left. |

| ใกล้ | Near. |

## Important Usage Notes

BTS and MRT are different rail systems. Their names should not be used interchangeably.

A place name may refer to several locations. An address or map pin can help identify the correct one.

“Can I walk there?” asks whether walking is possible. It does not establish route safety or accessibility.

The phrases in this document help users ask for directions. They do not provide verified navigation.

## Example Conversation

Visitor: ขอโทษค่ะ ห้องน้ำอยู่ที่ไหนคะ
English: Excuse me. Where is the restroom?

Local person: ตรงไป แล้วเลี้ยวขวาค่ะ
English: Go straight, then turn right.

Visitor: ขอบคุณค่ะ
English: Thank you.

## Example AI Response

User: What does “เลี้ยวซ้าย” mean?

Assistant: It means “turn left.” You may hear it when someone gives directions or tells a driver where to turn.

User: How do I ask whether I can walk there?

Thai: เดินไปได้ไหมครับ

English: Can I walk there?

Usage: Use this after identifying the destination. A female speaker can say เดินไปได้ไหมคะ.

## How This Supports the Project

The assistant can translate direction questions and explain short replies.

It should:
1. Identify the destination.
2. Ask for the starting point if actual navigation is requested.
3. Preserve place and station names.
4. Avoid inventing routes, exits, distances, or walking times.
5. Clearly distinguish phrase translation from navigation advice.
