# Shopping in Thailand

## Project: Lost in Translation — AI Thai Language Helper

## Purpose

This document helps foreigners ask about products, prices, sizes, colors, and payment when shopping in Thailand.

## Common Phrases

Add an appropriate polite ending: ครับ for male speakers, or ค่ะ / คะ for female speakers depending on the sentence.

| English | Thai | Situation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How much is this? | อันนี้เท่าไหร่ | Asking for a price. |
| Can you lower the price a little? | ลดหน่อยได้ไหม | Asking whether a discount is possible. |
| It is a little too expensive. | แพงไปหน่อย | Responding to a price. |
| Do you have a larger size? | มีไซซ์ใหญ่กว่านี้ไหม | Asking for a larger size. |
| Do you have a smaller size? | มีไซซ์เล็กกว่านี้ไหม | Asking for a smaller size. |
| Do you have other colors? | มีสีอื่นไหม | Asking about color options. |
| May I try it on? | ลองได้ไหม | Asking to try clothing or shoes. |
| I will take this one. | เอาอันนี้ | Choosing an item. |
| Can I pay by card? | จ่ายด้วยบัตรได้ไหม | Asking about payment. |
| A receipt, please. | ขอใบเสร็จด้วย | Requesting a receipt. |

## Important Usage Notes

### Asking about discounts

ลดหน่อยได้ไหม asks whether a discount is possible. It does not mean that bargaining is accepted in every shop.

### Trying an item

ลองได้ไหม broadly means “May I try it?” When pointing to clothing or shoes, it can mean “May I try it on?”

### Identifying the product

อันนี้ means “this one.” Point to the item when using it. If the seller cannot see the item, give its name or show a picture.

### Card payments

Ask the seller whether the intended card is accepted. The phrase does not establish accepted card types or payment conditions.

## Example Conversation

Customer: เสื้อตัวนี้เท่าไหร่คะ
English: How much is this shirt?

Seller: สามร้อยบาทค่ะ
English: Three hundred baht.

Customer: มีไซซ์ใหญ่กว่านี้ไหมคะ
English: Do you have a larger size?

Seller: มีค่ะ
English: Yes.

Customer: ลองได้ไหมคะ
English: May I try it on?

Customer: เอาอันนี้ค่ะ จ่ายด้วยบัตรได้ไหมคะ
English: I will take this one. Can I pay by card?

The price is an invented example, not a current market price.

## Example AI Response

User: How do I ask whether they have another color?

Thai: มีสีอื่นไหมครับ

English: Do you have other colors?

Usage: Point to the product when asking. A female speaker can say มีสีอื่นไหมคะ.

## How This Supports the Project

The assistant can identify whether the user needs help with price, size, color, selection, or payment.

It should:
1. Suggest a short and relevant Thai phrase.
2. Explain its meaning.
3. Use the product name when known.
4. Avoid promising discounts, refunds, stock availability, or card acceptance.
