# Ordering Food in Thailand

## Project: Lost in Translation — AI Thai Language Helper

## Purpose

This document helps foreigners order food in Thailand. It provides Thai phrases, English meanings, usage notes, and example conversations for the AI assistant's reference.

## Common Phrases

The phrases below use basic forms. Add ครับ for a male speaker. Female speakers commonly use ค่ะ for statements and requests, and คะ for questions.

| English | Thai | Situation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| May I see the menu? | ขอดูเมนูหน่อย | Asking for a menu. |
| I would like this one. | เอาอันนี้ | Pointing to a dish on the menu. |
| One plate of chicken fried rice, please. | ขอข้าวผัดไก่หนึ่งจาน | Ordering a meal. |
| Please do not add chili. | ไม่ใส่พริก | Requesting no added chili. |
| Only a little spicy, please. | เผ็ดนิดเดียว | Requesting a small amount of spice. |
| Please do not add pork. | ไม่ใส่หมู | Requesting no pork. |
| One bottle of drinking water, please. | ขอน้ำเปล่าหนึ่งขวด | Ordering water. |
| No ice, please. | ไม่ใส่น้ำแข็ง | Ordering a drink without ice. |
| To take away, please. | ใส่ถุงกลับบ้าน | Requesting takeaway food. |
| The bill, please. | เช็กบิลด้วย | Asking to pay. |

## Important Usage Notes

### No chili versus slightly spicy

ไม่ใส่พริก means “do not add chili.”

เผ็ดนิดเดียว means “only a little spicy.”

These requests are different. Prepared sauces or curry pastes may already contain chili, so a no-chili request does not guarantee that the food will contain no chili.

### Food preferences versus allergies

ไม่ใส่หมู only requests no pork. It does not mean vegetarian or vegan.

An ingredient request does not establish that a meal is safe for someone with an allergy. The customer must confirm ingredients and preparation with the staff.

## Example Conversation

Customer: ขอดูเมนูหน่อยค่ะ
English: May I see the menu?

Customer: ขอข้าวผัดไก่หนึ่งจานค่ะ ไม่ใส่พริกค่ะ
English: One plate of chicken fried rice, please. Do not add chili.

Staff: รับน้ำอะไรดีคะ
English: What would you like to drink?

Customer: ขอน้ำเปล่าหนึ่งขวดค่ะ
English: One bottle of drinking water, please.

Customer: เช็กบิลด้วยค่ะ
English: The bill, please.

## Example AI Response

User: How do I order chicken fried rice without chili?

Thai: ขอข้าวผัดไก่หนึ่งจานครับ ไม่ใส่พริกครับ

English: One plate of chicken fried rice, please. Do not add chili.

Usage: Say this when ordering. A female speaker can use ค่ะ instead of ครับ for this request.
